The Sprint

The 28-Day Zero-to-One Sprint

Week 1

Targeted Customer Discovery

I define the ideal user profile and conduct 3-5 in-depth customer interviews to validate core assumptions and identify actual pain points.

→ End of week deliverable: customer research synthesis and a validated problem definition.

Week 2

Strategy & Architecture

Distilling the interview data, I map the core user journeys and ruthlessly subtract bloat, sorting requirements into P0 must-haves and P1/P2 nice-to-haves to protect the engineering budget.

→ End of week deliverable: the lean MVP feature matrix.

Week 3

UX/UI & Systems Design

Translating the feature matrix into visual logic — end-to-end user flows and a scalable component library. We close the loop by testing the high-fidelity Figma prototype with 2-3 targeted users.

→ End of week deliverable: a validated, developer-ready Figma design system.

Week 4

Rapid Prototyping & Handoff

Moving beyond static files, I use AI-native tools to build a live, interactive web application prototype.

→ End of week deliverable: a functional, coded web prototype and a technical roadmap for your engineering team.
