About

Why I'm building this way

Over the past year I've been testing a thesis in production: that the convergence of product management, design, and AI-assisted engineering lets one person credibly own a lot more of the product lifecycle than the traditional team structure assumes. StockValue.Ai — a working financial analysis platform I designed and built solo using Bolt.new and Claude Opus — was the experiment. It worked well enough that I'm now taking on fractional and contract engagements built around the same model: research and strategy, a real Figma design system, and an AI-native build that goes live and keeps iterating.

I'm not selling speed for its own sake. The value is in the judgment — knowing which decisions need a human in the loop, which corners are safe to cut on a first pass, and how to structure a build so it's still maintainable six months later. That judgment is the same whether I'm coordinating firmware and mobile engineers at HP or prompting Claude inside Cursor.
